# Development timebox policy

Requested by operator on 2026-09-17.

For interactive implementation sessions, plan work in bounded workboxes of at most 28 minutes. At the start of a workbox, identify the concrete objective and expected stop condition. Stop earlier when a real operator action becomes the next gate. At the stop point, update project status/logs and report completed work, tests, blocking issues, and the next action.

This policy does not imply background execution after the chat response. Work is performed during the active implementation turn only.
